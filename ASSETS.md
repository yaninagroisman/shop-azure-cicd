## 3. Style guide for new or replacement images

**Style anchor.** Use this text word-for-word, so new images match the existing ones:

> *Cinematic photorealistic still, authentic Victorian magical joke-shop aesthetic in an old London lane, real practical props with tangible materials (aged brass, lacquered wood, hand-printed paper labels, waxed cotton, blown glass, enamel paint), warm tungsten and candlelight key light at about 3200K from the upper left, soft cool violet moonlight rim light from behind on the right, gentle atmospheric haze, rich colour palette of deep aubergine purple, burnt orange, burgundy, antique gold and parchment cream, shallow depth of field, subtle 35mm film grain, high dynamic range, ultra-detailed.*

**Negative prompt.** Add this to every image:

> *cartoon, illustration, 3d render look, plastic, CGI, anime, flat lighting, oversaturated neon, modern objects, logos of existing brands, any existing film or book franchise, watermark, signature, people, hands, garbled lettering, extra objects, cluttered background, blurry subject, low resolution*

**Colours** (they match the CSS tokens in [docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md)):

| Role | Hex values |
|---|---|
| Night | `#120612`, `#1b0a1c` |
| Purple | `#4a1a5e`, `#7a3b93` |
| Orange | `#d4612a`, `#f28c3a` |
| Burgundy | `#6b1d2c` |
| Gold | `#c99a3e`, `#f0cf7a` |
| Parchment | `#f3e6c8`, `#e6d2a6` |

**Lighting and camera.** Light every product the same way:

- warm key light from the upper left;
- violet rim light from the back right;
- the contact shadow falls down and to the right.

Shoot products at eye level, about 10° above, with a 100 mm macro look.

**Packaging lettering:**

- Use the product name and the small brand line **"Marvellous Mischief Co."**.
- Use the **M·M** monogram on seals.
- If the lettering comes out garbled, ask for an "ornate blank label". The name is always shown in HTML.

**Delivery:**

- Generate at 1600 px or larger and keep the PNG master in `optional-concepts/source-png/`.
- Cut out the product (see `optional-concepts/tools/README.md`).
- Place the product on a transparent 1600×1600 canvas, centred, with its base at 88% of the height.
- Export WebP (quality about 90) and a 640 px copy.
- Use the same file name as the image it replaces. No code change is needed.

**Before replacing an image, check:**

- [ ] Clean edges, with no white or black fringe.
- [ ] Same light direction and camera height as the other products.
- [ ] No people, no logos of existing brands, no garbled text.
- [ ] Multi-state images (`--extended`, `--open`, the fluff layers) line up with their base image.
- [ ] Replacing `storefront.webp` also means re-measuring the door, sign and glow positions in `main.css`.
